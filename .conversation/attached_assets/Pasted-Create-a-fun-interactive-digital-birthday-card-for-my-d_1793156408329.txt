SCREEN 3 — TWO TINY PUZZLES

The robot needs help repairing its birthday-card generator. Include two short, easy, interactive puzzles:

Puzzle 1: WORD REPAIR

Unscramble the letters THDAYBIR to complete the robot’s birthday greeting. Accept the correct answer regardless of capitalization and provide a hint if needed.

Puzzle 2: SECRET MESSAGE

Show a simple letter-substitution puzzle that reveals a birthday wish. Give a clear hint and accept the correct answer. Make the puzzle easy enough to solve in under a minute.

These should feel like tiny activities inside a birthday card, not levels in a game. Do not add scores, lives, timers, leaderboards, or complicated challenges.
